EXAMPLE — native Apple keyboard taps

These three rows come from measured clicks on Apple’s keyboard in the iOS 18.3.1 Simulator. They are not human participant data and are not the phone’s newly reported three taps.

The original measured source is simulator-ios18-direct-clicks.csv. Its three keyboard-region touch-downs were converted into the collector’s new taps.csv schema; this file was not generated by running the integrated collector. Keyboard-local positions equal measured screen positions minus the keyboard’s screen origin.

Apple’s key labels and per-key geometry are unknown and remain empty. Native text edits are recorded separately without guessed tap IDs. Apple’s original keyboard and prediction bar were active in the source experiment.

The physical iPhone probe CSV has since been received and verified separately: phone-native-keyboard-probe-1789135198.csv contains seven keyboard-region touch-downs and six text changes. This three-row example still comes only from the simulator source above. The integrated collector passed all 58 simulator tests, built successfully for signed device installation, and opened in the simulator. Its full on-device recording flow remains to be verified once the phone reconnects.
