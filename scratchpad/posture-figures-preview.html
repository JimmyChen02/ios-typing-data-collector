FreeTypeRecorder · Posture Guide

How to sit for a recording

Shown once after sign-up and re-openable from the menu. Two line-art figures make the rule read at a glance: sit tall, hold the phone up, keep your arm off the desk.

Do this

Back straight, phone held up, forearm free of the desk.

Not this

Slouched, forearm propped on the desk or table.

What ships in the app

Sit upright in a chair with your back straight. Hold the phone with the hand shown for each session. Keep your arm up — don’t rest it on a desk or table, and don’t lean on anything.

Figures are vector art: crisp at any size and re-tinted automatically for light and dark mode. The Ready? screen before each session also shows which hand (Left / Right / Both) to use that round.
